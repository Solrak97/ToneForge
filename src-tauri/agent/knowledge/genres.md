# ToneForge genre knowledge

## overview
Use Clean/Pushed for atmospheric and melodic work; Lead/Brown for metal. Prefer digital delay (`patch_delay_type_slot1=0`) and hall reverb (`patch_reverb_type_slot1=2`). Keep volumes in the user's preferred range.

## post-rock
God Is An Astronaut / Explosions vibe: Clean or Pushed, low-mid gain (32–45), light 2x2 chorus (`patch_fx_type_slot1=23`) with high direct_mix (~88–92), long digital delay (600–700ms, wet mix 40–50, feedback 45–55), huge hall reverb (level 50–60, time 60+). Gate off. Soft edge when digging in.

## shoegaze
Clean, chorus shimmer, analog/digital delay trails, wet hall. Avoid pitch-shifter silence traps.

## pink-floyd
Gilmour / Comfortably Numb: Clean or Lead. Soft compressor (`fx_type=3`). Digital delay ~480–560ms, moderate mix/feedback. Light hall. Mid-forward EQ. For solos: Lead gain ~55–65, stronger sustain, wetter delay. No tape wow.

## opeth
Clean melancholic: Clean amp, light chorus optional, hall reverb, soft mids.
Heavy (Ghost of Perdition): Brown, gain ~70–80, Metal Core booster (`booster_type=21`), mid-forward, NS gate on.
Cusp of Eternity solo: Lead gain ~64, compressor, digital delay ~420ms, light hall, gentle NS (low threshold) so sustain lives.

## jazz
Warm Jazz Clean: Clean amp, gain ~30–35, Warm OD off, soft compressor, short slap delay (~280ms low mix), spring reverb (`reverb_type=3`), dark treble. No heavy dirt.

## metal
Brown + Metal Core or HM-2 for buzzsaw. Tight NS. Mids for clarity. Delay/reverb minimal on rhythm.

## egyptian-metal
Brown, mid-forward, Metal Core light, digital delay ~360ms low mix, dark hall, NS on. Phrygian dominant playing style.

## king-crimson
Starless/Epitaph intro smooth: Clean, mid-forward, compressor, digital delay ~520ms, hall. Muted synth-like: Wave Synth (`fx_type=9`) low cutoff, Slow Gear (`fx_type=8` on FX slot), dark EQ, hall; delay optional.

## synth
Wave Synth + Slow Gear, dark Clean EQ, low cutoff, mostly synth_level with some direct_mix, hall reverb. Avoid pitch shifter direct_mix 0.

## dreamy
Lead or Clean, compressor, digital delay ~480ms, spacious hall, moderate gain, no wobble.

## ambient
Clean, dark EQ, long delay + wet hall, optional slow phaser carefully; keep dry signal present.
